# Everyinit name and logo

Everyinit is the name of this project. The Everyinit name, logo, and related
visual identity identify the official project and its official releases. This
notice does not claim that any mark is registered.

## Permitted references

You may use the name **Everyinit** in text to truthfully identify or link to
this project. You may also truthfully describe software as based on, derived
from, or compatible with Evin or ESH, provided that the description does not
suggest that the software is an official Everyinit release or is endorsed or
maintained by the Everyinit project.

The `logo.svg` and `favicon.svg` files in `.evin/actions/docs/` are original
Everyinit artwork. They are licensed under CC BY 4.0 as copyrightable works.
That copyright license permits copying and adaptation with attribution, but it
does not grant trademark rights in the Everyinit name or identity. In
particular, it does not authorize using the artwork to make an unofficial
release look official or to imply endorsement, affiliation, or maintenance by
Everyinit.

## Modified software and forks

If you distribute a modified version or fork of Evin or ESH, use your own
project name and visual identity as its primary branding. You may accurately
refer to Everyinit, Evin, or ESH in explanatory text (for example, “a fork of
Evin”), but do not name or present your distribution in a way that causes
people to mistake it for an official Everyinit project or release. Do not use
the Everyinit logo as the primary identity of a modified distribution.
